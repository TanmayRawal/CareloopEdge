# Glioma

Gliomas are primary brain tumors arising from glial or glial-precursor cells. They are the most common malignant primary brain tumors in adults. [CBTRUS]

The 2021 WHO classification of CNS tumours (CNS5) integrates histology with molecular markers. Adult-type diffuse gliomas comprise astrocytoma IDH-mutant, oligodendroglioma IDH-mutant and 1p/19q-codeleted, and glioblastoma IDH-wildtype, with CNS WHO grades 2 to 4. [WHO-CNS5]

On MRI, gliomas are intra-axial (within the brain tissue). Higher-grade gliomas typically show heterogeneous, often ring-like contrast enhancement with central necrosis and surrounding T2/FLAIR signal abnormality. Lower-grade gliomas are often non-enhancing and T2/FLAIR hyperintense. [EANO-glioma]

Imaging cannot establish the type, grade or molecular status of a glioma; diagnosis requires tissue (histopathology and molecular testing) and multidisciplinary review. [WHO-CNS5][EANO-glioma]
