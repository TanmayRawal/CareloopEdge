# Meningioma

Meningiomas arise from arachnoid cap cells of the meninges. They are the most common primary intracranial tumors, and most are CNS WHO grade 1. [CBTRUS][WHO-CNS5]

On MRI, a meningioma is typically an extra-axial, dural-based mass (outside the brain tissue) with usually homogeneous, avid contrast enhancement. A "dural tail" is common but not specific; a CSF cleft between tumor and brain supports an extra-axial location. [EANO-meningioma]

Many small, asymptomatic meningiomas are managed by observation with serial imaging; management decisions depend on size, growth, symptoms and patient factors and belong to the treating team. [EANO-meningioma]
