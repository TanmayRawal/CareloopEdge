# Pituitary tumor

Pituitary adenomas arise from the anterior pituitary gland in the sella turcica. The current WHO terminology is pituitary neuroendocrine tumor (PitNET). [PitNET]

By size, tumors under 10 mm are microadenomas and tumors of 10 mm or more are macroadenomas. Macroadenomas may extend above the sella and can compress the optic chiasm. [Melmed]

Dedicated pituitary MRI (thin-section coronal and sagittal T1-weighted images, often with dynamic contrast) is the standard imaging; a microadenoma often enhances less than the normal gland on early dynamic images. [Melmed]

Pituitary tumors can cause hormone excess or deficiency and visual symptoms; evaluation requires endocrine testing and specialist review, not imaging alone. [Melmed]

References: [PitNET] Asa SL et al. Overview of the 2022 WHO Classification of Pituitary Tumors. Endocr Pathol 2022;33:6-26. [Melmed] Melmed S. Pituitary-tumor endocrinopathies. N Engl J Med 2020;382:937-950.
