Intended use

Research and education. Not a medical device; not for clinical decisions.

Known limitations

  • Single 2D slices only; T1-weighted (contrast-enhanced) MRI. Other sequences and CT are rejected or unreliable.
  • Trained on public data (BRISC 2025, re-split to remove duplicate and same-patient leakage). Accuracy drops on data from other hospitals (external test macro-F1 ≈ 0.84).
  • For pituitary tumors the classifier relies largely on surrounding anatomy rather than the lesion itself; the lesion outline comes from a separate segmentation head.

How the explanations work

Grad-CAM (exact): for this architecture the map is an exact decomposition — its values sum to the model's score for the class. Finer-CAM shows what separates the predicted class from the runner-up. Deep Explain (LayerCAM) is a sharper, less faithful detail view. Explanations show what the model used, not where the disease is.
